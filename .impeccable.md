## Design Context

### Users
Growing business owners and operational leaders who need faster quoting, invoicing, expense tracking, reporting, and clearer visibility into profit, cash, and performance. They are evaluating FekiTech as a dependable system that can reduce administrative friction and support scalable growth.

### Brand Personality
Confident, structured, and intelligent. The experience should communicate premium operational discipline without feeling cold, flashy, or overly technical.

### Aesthetic Direction
A refined dark SaaS presentation with cinematic restraint, generous spacing, crisp typography, and selective electric blue and violet accents. Preserve the existing FekiTech identity and strong sections while improving hierarchy, responsiveness, and polish. Avoid noisy effects, generic purple SaaS styling, excessive glass, and heavy animation.

### Design Principles
1. Clarity before decoration: every visual treatment must improve comprehension or emphasis.
2. Premium through restraint: use generous rhythm, controlled line lengths, and subtle depth rather than visual noise.
3. Mobile is an adapted experience: maintain readable hero content, comfortable touch targets, and clean single-column card flow.
4. Brand accents stay selective: blue and violet identify important words, actions, and system states.
5. Performance is part of the design: reserve media dimensions, reduce motion when requested, and avoid unnecessary scripts or layout shifts.
